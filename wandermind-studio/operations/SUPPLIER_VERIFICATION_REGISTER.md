## Copy-and-send supplier questions

```text
Hello, WanderMind is checking one experience for a real traveller itinerary. Please reply with information valid for the requested travel date:

1. Legal operator/business name and venue address
2. Current licence/permit details relevant to this activity
3. Insurance coverage and what it covers
4. Instructor/driver/guide qualification
5. Safety briefing, equipment, age/health/fitness restrictions
6. Exact package, duration, pickup area and inclusions
7. Current price, currency, taxes and extra fees
8. Availability for the requested date
9. Weather/closure and cancellation/refund rules
10. Booking contact or official booking URL

Please confirm the date of this information. WanderMind will not call the experience execution-ready until these points are checked.
```

## Source boundary

- Supplier websites are treated as supplier self-report, not independent proof of licensing or insurance.
- Association or government directory listings confirm context/listing only unless they explicitly show the required current credential.
- Advertised prices are not copied into the route quote while pages conflict or no operator is bound to the route.
- The two area/name POIs `thousand_islands_viewpoint` and `mount_batur_trailhead` remain `pending_review`; they are not supplier records and require exact identity/entrance confirmation.
