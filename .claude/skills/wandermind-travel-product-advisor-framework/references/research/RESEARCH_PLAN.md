# Quick-tier research plan

Cutoff date: 2026-08-02

## Scope

- Audience: WanderMind founder and implementation agent.
- Decisions: route product structure, low-cost paid validation, referral rewards,
  driver-linked pricing, content trust, and operational risk.
- Out of scope: invented driver prices, medical claims, copied venue assets,
  and public-figure impersonation.

## Three quick-tier evidence dimensions

1. Product experience and simplicity.
2. Startup validation, pricing, and referral economics.
3. Travel operations, trust, safety, and supplier constraints.

Each dimension is limited to five high-value sources. Primary sources and
project evidence take priority. Research findings remain separate until the
quality checkpoint is accepted.
